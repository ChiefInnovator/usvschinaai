# Developer Levels at Microsoft, Google, Meta, OpenAI, and Anthropic

Developer levels describe **how independently someone works, the complexity they handle, and the scope of their impact**. This comparison covers the commonly reported progression for engineers on the individual contributor track, rather than management.

Sources checked September 7, 2026. Public reporting is not a complete official internal career framework, and comparisons between companies are approximate.

| Company | Levels, from lower to higher |
|---|---|
| **Microsoft** | **59–60:** Software Engineer → **61–62:** Software Engineer II → **63–64:** Senior → **65–67:** Principal → **68–69:** Partner → **70:** Distinguished Engineer → **80:** Technical Fellow. [Source](https://d3h68kq65nkuzu.cloudfront.net/company/Microsoft/salaries/Software-Engineer/Principal-SDE/Greater-Seattle-Area/) |
| **Google** | **L3:** Software Engineer II → **L4:** Software Engineer III → **L5:** Senior → **L6:** Staff → **L7:** Senior Staff → **L8:** Principal → **L9:** Distinguished → **L10:** Google Fellow. [Source](https://d3h68kq65nkuzu.cloudfront.net/?compare=Apple%2CGoogle) |
| **Meta** | **E3:** Entry → **E4:** Mid-level → **E5:** Senior → **E6:** Staff → **E7:** Senior Staff → **E8:** Principal → **E9:** Distinguished. These are commonly used external title mappings. [Source](https://www.resumeadapter.com/companies/meta/levels) |
| **OpenAI** | Public compensation reports list **L2 → L3 → L4 → L5 → L6**. A complete official mapping to Senior, Staff, and Principal titles wasn’t verified. [Source](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/openai/salaries/software-engineer) |
| **Anthropic** | Public job postings include **Software Engineer → Senior Software Engineer → Staff Software Engineer → Senior Staff Software Engineer**. A complete numbered internal ladder wasn’t verified. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/careers/jobs?hsLang=en) |

## Typical Responsibilities

| Career stage | Typical responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Entry / Junior** | Completes defined tasks with guidance. |
| **Mid-level** | Independently builds and maintains features. |
| **Senior** | Owns complex projects, makes design decisions, and mentors others. |
| **Staff / Principal** | Leads technical work across teams and shapes architecture. |
| **Distinguished / Fellow** | Sets technical direction across the company or influences the industry. |

**Titles aren’t directly interchangeable:** Microsoft’s Principal title starts earlier in its ladder than Google’s Principal title. Compare responsibilities and scope alongside the title.
